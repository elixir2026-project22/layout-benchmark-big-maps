Layout benchmark - big maps

Grouping big pathway maps, and distributing the elements inside each group and the groups themselves. BioHackathon Europe 2026, project 22 (Open Pathway Maps). Code.

  • grouping - 52 pictures: every model under every grouping strategy, drawn by one renderer (54 files, 13 MB)
  • layout - 288 pictures: 8 layout options x 9 groupings x 4 big models (290 files, 87 MB)

Each cell links the full-size SVG. The pictures are the repository's own examples/ and layout/examples/, copied unchanged.
