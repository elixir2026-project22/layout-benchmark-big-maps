Grouping layouts — examples

Each picture is one grouping of one model, laid out by one layout option. The option is run at every level of the grouping: it places the species and reactions inside a leaf group, the sub-groups inside a group, and the top-level groups themselves (layout/placement.py), and the picture is drawn by the same renderer as the grouping gallery (layout/render_layout.py): species coloured by compartment, reactions by SBO term, compartment levels drawn as a cell, boundary nodes in a dashed band, edges that leave their top-level group in red. A group holding one entity and nothing else is not drawn as a group - its node is placed in the parent instead, counted per cell as ungrouped singletons. Click a picture for the full-size SVG.

Options (one per family of layout-benchmark's registry): graphviz-dot (layered), igraph-sugiyama (layered (stands in for tree)), graphviz-neato (stress), graphviz-sfdp (force-directed), igraph-drl (for large graphs), graphviz-twopi (radial), graphviz-circo (circular), igraph-mds (baseline). Not available on the machine that built this page: pathway-specific (rules-claude, ecmapper, metacarto, sbml2map - need their checkouts under .tools/); SBML-specific (sbmlnetwork - needs its checkout under .tools/); compartments as groups (fcose / cose-bilkent / reagraph compound layouts - need Node); compound-capable force-directed (fcose, cose-bilkent - need Node); stress, with constraints (cola - needs Node); tree (elk-mrtree - needs Node; igraph-sugiyama stands in as the hierarchical option).
